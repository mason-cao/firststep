# First Step Team Website

## Register
Brand

## Users
Primary users are community families in Metro Atlanta: students, parents, and local community members who want to understand whether First Step Team is active, trustworthy, and worth joining or following. Secondary users include partner nonprofits, venues, grant reviewers, sponsors, and local organizations looking for a reliable youth-led collaborator.

## Purpose
The site is an impact-first public record for a youth-led nonprofit. It should communicate service, environmental action, culture, youth leadership, and measurable community impact without feeling like a generic nonprofit template.

## Strategic Principles
- Lead with a narrative, then prove it quickly with public impact metrics.
- Preserve the full crawl/content inventory while revising weak legacy copy into a clearer story.
- Make impact records useful and scannable: ledgers, timelines, public data modules, and archive tables.
- Feature leadership first, then alumni, then the full member roster in a lower-emphasis archive.
- Treat sponsorship as a trust signal, not the main story.

## Tone
Warm, rigorous, civic, energetic, and mature. The site should feel built by students with real momentum, not by a corporate fundraising department.

## Anti-References
Avoid generic blue nonprofit templates, repeated icon-card grids, dark teal/orange institutional palettes, glassmorphism, centered hero stacks, and decorative motion that does not clarify the story.
